NeurIPS 2022 / Self-supervised

SatMAE

A masked autoencoder pretraining framework for learning from temporal and multispectral satellite imagery.

Short Summary

SatMAE adapts masked autoencoding to satellite imagery, using temporal structure and spectral band groups to pretrain stronger remote-sensing representations.

Key Contribution

The paper introduces temporal embeddings, independent masking across time, and spectral positional encodings for multispectral inputs.

Why It Matters

It helped establish masked image modeling as a practical self-supervised path for Earth observation tasks with limited labels.

Key Contributions

Temporal pretraining Uses temporal embeddings and masking strategies designed for satellite image time series.
Multispectral pretraining Groups spectral bands and gives them spectral positional encodings.
Transfer learning Reports gains on downstream land cover classification and semantic segmentation tasks.
