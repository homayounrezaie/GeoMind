AlphaEarth Foundations

A geospatial AI model that integrates Earth observation data into compact embeddings for global mapping and monitoring.

What It Is

AlphaEarth Foundations acts like a virtual satellite: it combines diverse Earth observation sources into a unified representation for terrestrial land and coastal waters.

Core Output

The system creates compact 64-dimensional embedding fields, including annual embeddings released through Google Earth Engine as the Satellite Embedding dataset.

Primary Uses

The model supports ecosystem classification, agriculture and environmental change analysis, deforestation monitoring, urban expansion tracking, and water resource assessment.

Model Details

Category Foundation Model
Organization Google DeepMind / Google Earth Engine
Spatial unit 10x10 m land and coastal water squares
Embedding size 64 components
Dataset access Satellite Embedding dataset in Google Earth Engine
Reported result 24% lower error rate on average than tested alternatives
