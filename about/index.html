About GeoMind

GeoMind is a structured hub for Geospatial AI. It helps researchers, engineers, and practitioners follow important models, datasets, benchmarks, and papers in one clean place.

GeoMind organizes Geospatial AI into visual cards for models, datasets, benchmarks, and papers. The goal is to make the field easier to scan, compare, and follow while keeping readers close to original sources.
