Navigate GeoAI with clarity.

Every day a new foundation model is released, every week another paper redefines what’s possible with satellite imagery, SAR, or LiDAR, and every month a new benchmark shifts the state of the art. Keeping up is genuinely hard!!! not because the work isn’t remarkable, but because without understanding the full structure of the field, each new model or paper feels like a disconnected event. You can’t tell if it’s foundational or incremental, you can’t see where it fits, what it replaces, or what it makes possible next.

GeoMind maps the entire landscape of geospatial AI, from raw sensor data and learning paradigms through model architectures and key techniques to tasks, datasets, stack, companies, and standards, with each layer linking directly to relevant resources so you always know where you are, what connects, and where to go next.
