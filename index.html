Every day a new foundation model is released, every week another paper redefines what is possible with satellite imagery, SAR, or LiDAR, and every month a new benchmark shifts the state of the art. Keeping up is genuinely hard!!! not because the work is not remarkable, but because without understanding the full structure of the field, each new model or paper feels like a disconnected event. You cannot tell if it is foundational or incremental, where it fits, what it replaces, or what it makes possible next.

GeoMind maps the landscape from sensor data and learning paradigms through model architectures, techniques, tasks, datasets, tools, companies, and standards, then connects each layer to public resources so you know where you are and where to go next.
